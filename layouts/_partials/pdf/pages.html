{{- /* Returns the child pages of a section that belong in the PDF, in sidebar order. Both walks in pdf/render.html (contents, then content) call this, so the section numbers they generate can't drift apart. Excluded: no_print the flag Docsy's print output already honours manuallink sidebar-only entries that redirect elsewhere. They carry no content of their own — printing them yields a numbered heading followed by nothing. toc_hide kept out of the site's own navigation, so out of the book's hide_summary table of contents too. This is what excludes the Search dontIndex page, whose content is a search form with nothing to print. Excluding a section excludes everything beneath it, so a flag on an _index.md drops that whole branch. None of the pages currently flagged have children; check that before adding one that does. @context {page} . The section to list. @returns {page.Pages} */ -}} {{- $pages := slice -}} {{- range (union .Pages .Sections).ByWeight -}} {{- if and (ne .Params.no_print true) (not .Params.manuallink) (ne .Params.toc_hide true) (ne .Params.hide_summary true) (ne .Params.dontIndex true) -}} {{- $pages = $pages | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $pages -}}
